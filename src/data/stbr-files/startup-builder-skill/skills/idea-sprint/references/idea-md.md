# `.claude/context/idea.md` format

idea-sprint writes this file; pitch-deck and hackathon read it. Keep it short, factual and dated. Overwrite scalars on a re-run; append to the evidence list rather than deleting it.

```markdown
# Idea
Updated: YYYY-MM-DD
Decision: go | conditional | no-go   (confidence 0.0–1.0)

## The idea
- One-liner: <what it does, for whom, in words a non-crypto person follows>
- Target user: <a role or community you can name>
- Wedge: <the niche entry point>
- Why crypto: <the load-bearing reason from the crypto-necessity test>
- Why Solana: <one concrete reason>

## Scores (/15)
| Founder fit | MVP speed | Distribution | Market pull | Revenue path | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Demand evidence
- YYYY-MM-DD <signal> — strong | weak — <link or source>

## Bear case
- Dies because: <one sentence>
- Survives because: <one sentence>

## Runners-up
- <name>: <score> — <why it lost>

## Open risks and next step
- <the one dimension to de-risk first, or the pivot if no-go>
```

Never put a number in "Demand evidence" that you cannot link or name a source for.
