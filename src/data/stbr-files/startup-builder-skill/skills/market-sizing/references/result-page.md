# Wedge map result page

The page other people read: principles and the result. Process, search logs, corrections,
version history and supporting research stay out; the research note is linked, not pasted.
Render it, on request, as one self-contained HTML file: inline CSS, no fonts, scripts or
images fetched from elsewhere.

## Structure

1. **Thesis title:** the sentence the ranking proves, in the customer's words.
2. **How it was ranked,** three to five lines: each filter in one line; Q×A in one line; D
   only breaks ties; rejected only where it cannot work.
3. **The top N:** a table of # · candidate · Q · A · Q×A · D · why (one line) · first step.
4. **Rejected, in buckets:** each bucket with its reason in one line and the list of names.
5. **Caveat:** "A conversation with a buyer decides among the top N." Desk scores are
   `[inferred]`.
6. **Sources:** one link to the research note, not the sources themselves.

Before sharing, read only the headings in order, and cut anything that doesn't change the
reader's decision.
